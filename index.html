01 · Current challenges

Losses are hard to see, locate and fix

01

No dedicated loss monitoring system

Loss data is scattered across different systems with no single unified view.

It is hard to pinpoint exactly where losses occur across the distribution network.

02

Limited real-time visibility

Limited ability to detect losses at feeder, transformer and customer level.

Problem-solving is reactive, after the fact, rather than preventive.

03

Manual, disconnected analysis

Analysing and reconciling data takes a long time across separate systems, which delays detecting and resolving energy loss.

TodayReactive response after incidents
With ELMSProactive detection and prevention
02 · Project objectives

Five goals for energy loss management

1Accurately calculate energy loss at each level of the network
2Separate Technical Loss from Non-Technical Loss
3Detect abnormal consumption and possible electricity theft early
4Improve the accuracy of energy loss calculation
5Support planning to improve the network and reduce losses

03 · Solution overview

One module, one view of loss

Unified management through OMMD

ELMS is a module built inside OMMD, providing unified management and comprehensive loss monitoring across the whole network.

Automated analysis

Advanced automated analysis and near real-time monitoring detect anomalies early and continuously improve performance.

Single source of truth

Consolidated data keeps loss figures consistent across departments and supports decisions based on clear information.
